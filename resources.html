FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know.

Common questions about TALENTIQ AI recruitment engine, security, and candidate matching.

What is AI recruitment? +
AI recruitment uses artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms to automate resume screening, score candidate suitability, match applicant profiles against job specifications, and streamline hiring workflows.
How does candidate matching work? +
TALENTIQ analyzes job descriptions and candidate resumes using natural language processing (NLP) to generate a match score percentage (e.g., 94%) based on skill overlap, experience depth, and role requirements.
Can recruiters review AI recommendations? +
Yes, absolutely. TALENTIQ operates under a "Human-in-the-Loop" architecture where AI provides transparent scorecards and recommendations while human recruiters retain 100% final decision authority.
How is candidate data protected? +
Candidate data is protected using enterprise-grade AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit, backed by SOC2 compliant infrastructure and strict role-based access controls.
Can teams collaborate on candidates? +
Yes, team members can share candidate profiles, submit standardized interview scorecards, leave real-time feedback comments, and track candidate pipeline progress together.
Can the platform support multiple job roles? +
Yes, TALENTIQ supports unlimited concurrent job openings, customized candidate pipelines per department, and multi-tenant recruiter permissions.
How does recruitment analytics work? +
The platform aggregates pipeline metrics automatically, offering interactive SVG visualizations for time-to-hire, funnel stage conversion rates, candidate sourcing channels, and interviewer productivity.
Does the platform support enterprise hiring teams? +
Yes, TALENTIQ provides enterprise-grade governance including admin audit logs, organization hierarchy management, single sign-on (SSO), and custom ATS API integrations.
