FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About TALENTIQ.

A quick overview of how the platform supports modern recruitment teams.

What does TALENTIQ help recruiters manage?

TALENTIQ brings role creation, candidate sourcing, AI-assisted screening, talent matching, interviews, collaboration, and recruitment analytics into one workflow.

Does AI make the final hiring decision?

No. AI-assisted signals are designed to support recruiter workflows while human teams retain control over final candidate decisions.

Can multiple recruiters collaborate on the same role?

Yes. Teams can work around shared candidate information, interview feedback, scorecards, and recruitment progress.

Can TALENTIQ support multiple open positions?

The platform experience is designed around multiple roles, candidate pipelines, hiring teams, and recruitment activities running simultaneously.

What makes the platform useful for hiring teams?

It connects candidate intelligence, workflow automation, team collaboration, and recruitment analytics so teams can work from a shared hiring view.
